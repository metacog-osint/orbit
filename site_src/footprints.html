Footprints

Where a communications satellite's beams reach the ground. Every outline says which kind it is, because the kinds are not equally reliable.

Kinds of outline

  • Published. A contour the operator filed with a regulator or published itself, drawn as given, with its source and date.
  • Computed. Exact geometry from the satellite's position: where it stands above a stated angle over the horizon. It says where a signal could reach, not where a beam points.
  • Estimated. A beam drawn from stated assumptions, such as a filed antenna gain or a named service area. Its error is unknown unless a published contour of the same beam is there to check it against; where one is, the scores are shown.
  • Measured. Places where a terminal on the network was seen in use. A lower bound: it shows where service works, and its absence shows nothing.
